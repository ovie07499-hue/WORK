# Responsible academic use

Academic Style Refiner is an **editing** tool. It changes how a text says something, not
what it says, and it is designed for writing you are entitled to submit.

## Appropriate uses

- Improving the flow, variety and clarity of your own drafts, including drafts written in a
  second language.
- Removing repetitive or formulaic phrasing before you submit a manuscript, thesis chapter
  or report.
- Editing team documents that you co-author, with your co-authors' agreement.

## Uses it is not intended for

- Rewording someone else's work so that it looks like your own. Paraphrasing does not
  change authorship: ideas, findings and structure still need to be credited, and a
  rewritten passage taken from a source is still plagiarism without a citation.
- Concealing how a text was produced from people who are entitled to know, such as
  instructors, examiners, editors or reviewers, where their rules require disclosure.
- Producing content: the tool adds no ideas, evidence, data or references, and it should
  not be relied on to do so.

## Your responsibilities

1. **Check the rules that apply to you.** Universities, courses, journals and funders have
   different policies on AI-assisted editing. Some allow it freely, some require a
   disclosure statement, and some prohibit it for assessed work. Follow the strictest rule
   that applies.
2. **Disclose where required.** A typical statement: *"An AI-based editing tool (Academic
   Style Refiner, using Claude) was used to improve the language and style of this
   manuscript. The author reviewed all edits and takes full responsibility for the content."*
3. **Read every paragraph.** The pipeline masks citations and checks numbers, but a
   rewrite can still shift emphasis, hedging or nuance in ways a program cannot detect. The
   side-by-side view, "Keep original" and "Regenerate" exist so that you stay in control.
4. **You remain the author.** You are accountable for every claim in the final text,
   whichever tool helped you phrase it.
5. **Mind confidentiality.** Text is sent to a third-party language model for processing.
   Don't submit material you are not allowed to share (unpublished data under embargo,
   personal data, peer-review manuscripts you are reviewing) unless your institution has
   approved this use.

## What the tool does and does not guarantee

- It **does** keep citations, numeric references, URLs, DOIs, inline maths and direct
  quotations byte-for-byte, and it rejects rewrites that drop or alter numbers.
- It **does not** guarantee that meaning is perfectly preserved, that the result meets a
  particular style guide, or how any third-party software will classify the text. It is
  not built or marketed to defeat AI-detection or plagiarism-detection systems.
